Why prices differ by city

  • Freight: the distance from the factory or warehouse changes the delivered price.
  • Local taxes and charges can differ from one city to another.
  • Dealer margins and stock levels vary by city.
  • Quantity: bulk orders are often priced differently from single pieces.

Tips before you buy

  • Check the price in your own city before you decide.
  • Compare two or three brands for the same product.
  • Look at what is included, such as warranty and installation.
  • Confirm the final price with the seller, since listed prices can change.

Frequently asked questions

Are the prices the same in every city?

Not always. Prices can differ by city because of local dealers, freight and taxes. Open your city below to see its price pages.

How do I find the price of a product in my city?

Pick your city and a product in the search box above, or open a city below and choose the product.

Is my city listed?

We list 49 cities today. If yours is missing, use the Pan-India Prices view and confirm the final price with the seller.

Are these final prices?

Prices are for guidance. Please confirm the final price with the seller before buying.
