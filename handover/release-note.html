Dev release note: static category and price pages

2026-11-22. In-house. Read this first.

Read in this order: this note, the mockups, dev-handover.html (all page types on one sheet), content-template.html (copy templates), structured-data-reference.html (schema examples).

1. What to build

  • One base template, rendered from data. The product cards are the same on every page. The strip above the cards and the strips after the View All button change by page type.
  • Everything that changes per category is a {{token}}. Tick the box at the bottom right of any mockup to see them (purple). Orange blocks are the same on every page.
  • Pages: category pages (L2 and L3), price pages (national, city, brand, brand + city, range, capacity, type) and one price hub.

4. The price hub is one page

  • The price hub is one common page at /price/. Pan-India Prices, In My City and Brand Prices are toggle views inside that page, not separate pages or URLs.
  • All three views are in the HTML (JavaScript only hides the ones not selected), so every link is crawlable.
  • The toggle is in the same place on the hub: centred under the site header, above the hero. Do not repeat it lower on the page.

5. Breadcrumbs

  1. Start with Home.
  2. Show every level above the page that has its own page.
  3. The last crumb is the page H1 and is not a link. Every other crumb links to a real page.
  4. The visible breadcrumb and the BreadcrumbList schema must match.

6. Internal links

Every page links up to its parent, down to its children, and across to its siblings. A category page and its national price page link to each other. All links are real <a href> in the HTML, only to pages that exist. Do not link filtered app URLs (?l3_category_ids=...).

8. Structured data

See structured-data-reference.html. Every page has a BreadcrumbList, a CollectionPage with an ItemList of its links, and a FAQPage that matches the visible FAQs. No Product markup on listing pages.

9. Publishing rule

  • Build the template for every category. Publish a category page only if it passes the size rule: at least 30 products to start.
  • Pages below the limit are noindex, left out of the sitemap and not linked from strips. They switch on when they pass.
  • Category pages do not target price. Price pages own the price keyword. Each page has its own self-canonical.
  • 24 product cards on category pages, 12 on price pages. Keep each page's HTML under 2 MB.

10. Ideas pages

Ideas L2 pages (live example: /idea/residential/modern-bedroom-designs.html) are in-house. They use a similar template, with larger card images because the audience is image-led. A mockup will follow.
