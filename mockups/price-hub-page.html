Why prices differ

  • Brand and model: the same product type is sold at different price points.
  • Material, size and finish change the price of most building products.
  • City: freight, local taxes and dealer margins can change what you pay.
  • Quantity: bulk orders are often priced differently from single pieces.

Tips before you buy

  • Check the price in your own city before you decide.
  • Compare two or three brands for the same product.
  • Look at what is included, such as warranty and installation.
  • Confirm the final price with the seller, since listed prices can change.

Frequently asked questions

Are the prices the same in every city?

Not always. Prices can differ by city because of local dealers, freight and taxes. Use the city view to check your own city.

How do I find the price of a product from a particular brand?

Open the brand view, choose the brand and pick the product. Each brand page lists the products it covers.

Are these final prices?

Prices are for guidance. Please confirm the final price with the seller before buying.

My product or city is not listed. What should I do?

Use the search box to find the nearest product. We add new products and cities as they become available.
