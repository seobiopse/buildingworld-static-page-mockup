Content templates: category and price pages

BuildingWorld static pages. Updated 2026-11-22. Copy the template text and let the data fill the {{tokens}}.

Writing rules. FAQ answers start by restating the question. Category pages do not target price. Price pages own the price keyword. Meta title about 60 characters, meta description about 155 characters. Plain text only, no price claims that are not in the data. Token list: see the Tokens sheet in the Dev Handover.
