Dev Handover: BuildingWorld static category and price pages

Updated 2026-11-21. Mockups are in /mockups. Sample values are marked in the Open Items sheet.

How to use: one base template, two page types. The product cards are the same everywhere. The strip above the cards and the strips after the View All button change by page type. Purple {{tokens}} are filled from data; plain text is hard-coded. Open the mockups in /mockups and tick the box at the bottom right to see the tokens on the page. Start with the Page Types and Overlap sheets.

#RuleWhat it meansExample
11Separate keywordsCategory page title, H1 and meta do not target price. Price pages own the price keyword.Category: Air Conditioner. Price: ac price.
22Different lead contentCategory pages lead with types, products and brands. Price pages lead with price data and lowest price first.Category 24 cards, default sort. Price 12 cards, lowest price first.
33FAQsCategory FAQs cover brands, type, key attribute, quotes. One price pointer FAQ. Price FAQs cover price.Category FAQ: Where can I see the price of an air conditioner?
44Links acrossCategory page links to its national price page in the bottom block only (FAQ pointer and price-list link). Price page links back once, in the View All button. Strips link only within their own type.No price links in the category page strips.
55No canonical between themEach page has a self-referencing canonical. They are different pages.Category canonical = category URL; price canonical = /price/<slug>.
66Strips by page typeCategory page: category + city. Price page: price + city, brand + price, brand + city + price.See Links sheet.
77City pagesA category + city page exists only where the city has real local dealers and enough products.Price city pages already filtered by the Dev Handover.
