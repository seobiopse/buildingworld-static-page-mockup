Common to every page

BreadcrumbSame pattern as the live pages: Home > Products > L1 > L2 > page for products (live example /product/floors-and-walls/wall-tiles.html) and Home > Ideas > category > page for ideas (live example /idea/residential/modern-bedroom-designs.html). Price pages follow the hub link map: Home > Price > product > brand and/or city, or > price range. The last crumb is the page H1 and is not a link. The visible crumbs and the BreadcrumbList schema must match.
CanonicalOwn absolute URL, https://www.buildingworld.ai/...
robotsindex, follow, max-image-preview:large (noindex below the minimum product count)
html langen-IN
Open Graphog:type website; og:site_name BuildingWorld; og:locale en_IN; og:title = Title; og:description = Meta description; og:url = canonical; og:image = first product image on the page (a dedicated 1200 x 630 image is better); og:image:alt = Title without '| BuildingWorld'
Twittertwitter:card summary_large_image; twitter:title, twitter:description, twitter:image = the og values
SchemaBreadcrumbList; CollectionPage with a URL-only ItemList of the product URLs on the page; FAQPage with the same text as the visible FAQs. No Product, Offer or AggregateOffer on listing pages.
LengthTitle about 60 characters; meta description about 155 characters

Notes

  • Shop by Price Range is part of the base category template: range chips (Under ₹X, ₹X – ₹Y, Above ₹Z) link to the range pages; View all price links to the category's national price page.
  • Ranges come from the category's price distribution (AC example from the live filter: Under ₹70,000 = 154, ₹70,000 – ₹1,27,000 = 16, ₹1,27,000 – ₹1,85,000 = 6, Above ₹1,85,000 = 5).
  • Category pages do not target the price keyword. Price pages own it. Each page is self-canonical. Do not link filtered app URLs (?l3_category_ids=...).
  • Tokens: tick the box at the bottom right of any mockup to see every {{token}}. Copy text for titles, meta, H1, intros and FAQs is in content-template.html.
  • Open: static L1 and L2 URL pattern; domain .ai vs .com; 13 slugs marked 'confirm' in the Dev Handover.
