Notes

  • Domain: https://www.buildingworld.ai. Canonicals, og:url and schema URLs are absolute on that domain.
  • L1 categories stay in the application: there is no static L1 page. L2 and L3 are static pages.
  • The price hub is one page with a toggle (Pan-India Prices, In My City, Brand Prices). The views are not separate pages. All three are in the HTML.
  • Breadcrumb: the last crumb is the page H1 and is not a link. The visible crumbs and the BreadcrumbList schema must match.
  • Capacity pages and tables are not in the mockups for now. They will be decided after other categories are built.
  • Every {{token}} is shown in purple in the mockups (tick the box at the bottom right). Copy text is in content-template.html.
  • Open: category in location URL and which cities qualify; demand check for range and type pages; the L2 URL slugs; 13 slugs marked "confirm" in the Dev Handover.
